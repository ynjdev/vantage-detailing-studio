FORMAT
------
.webp is best (much smaller than JPG at the same quality).
Convert free at squoosh.app — drag in, pick WebP, download.
.jpg works too, but rename the reference in the page if you use it.

Keep each file under 400 KB. Big images make the site slow, and Google
penalises slow sites in local search.

SHOOTING BEFORE / AFTER PROPERLY
--------------------------------
The whole credibility of a before/after rests on it being honest:

  - Same angle. Mark where you stand.
  - Same light. Same time of day, same torch position.
  - Same panel. Not the bonnet before and the door after.
  - Don't shoot the before in shade and the after in sun.

A single-source light held at a low angle across the panel (raking light)
is what makes swirls visible. Phone torch works fine.

Shoot the before BEFORE you wash it. Once it's washed, half the story is
already gone.
