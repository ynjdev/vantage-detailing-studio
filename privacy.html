Privacy Policy

Last updated: —

Vantage Detail Studio is a mobile vehicle detailing business operating in Johannesburg, South Africa. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and what we do with it, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What We Collect

We only collect what we need to quote for and carry out detailing work:

  • Contact details — your name, phone number and email address
  • Service address — where the vehicle is, so we can get to it
  • Vehicle details — make, model, registration, colour and paint condition
  • Job and payment records — quotes, invoices, what was done and what was paid

We do not collect ID numbers, and we do not ask for or store your card details. Online card payments are handled entirely by our payment provider on their own systems — your card number never reaches us.

Why We Collect It

  • To quote accurately and confirm bookings
  • To reach the right address at the right time
  • To recommend the right correction level and coating for your paint
  • To issue invoices and reconcile payments
  • To let you know when a ceramic coating is due for renewal
  • To keep the records a business is required to keep for tax purposes

Who Can See It

Access is limited to the Vantage Detail Studio team members who need it to do their jobs. Every account has its own login, and access is logged.

We share information with third parties only where necessary to run the business:

  • Our payment provider — to process online payments
  • Our hosting provider — which stores the data on our behalf

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Marketing

We will contact you about the job you booked. We will only send you promotional messages if you have agreed to receive them, and you can tell us to stop at any time — just reply and say so, and we will action it.

A reminder that your ceramic coating is nearing the end of its life is service-related, not marketing, but you can opt out of those too.

How Long We Keep It

We keep job and payment records for as long as SARS requires records to be retained. Contact and vehicle details are kept while you remain a client and for a reasonable period afterwards, in case you come back. If you would rather we deleted your details sooner, ask us and we will, except where we are legally required to keep a record of a transaction.

Keeping It Secure

Our systems use encrypted connections, individually password-protected accounts, and access logging. No system is perfect, but we take reasonable steps to protect what we hold, and we do not keep information we do not need.

Your Rights

Under POPIA you can:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct anything that is wrong
  • Ask us to delete information we no longer need
  • Object to us using your information for marketing
  • Complain to the Information Regulator if you think we have got it wrong

To exercise any of these, contact us using the details below and we will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

Cookies

Our website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. If you use our online payment page, a single session cookie keeps your session secure while you are on it. That is all.
