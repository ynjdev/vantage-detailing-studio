How It Works In Benoni

What To Expect

We arrive at your address in Benoni with everything needed — polisher, lighting, chemicals, water containment. All we need from you is access to a tap and a power point near the vehicle.

A single-stage polish takes 2–4 hours. Multi-stage correction runs 5–7. Ceramic coating adds cure time on top, during which the car must stay dry — so we plan the booking around the weather.

Before we leave, we walk the car with you under raking light so you can see the correction yourself.
