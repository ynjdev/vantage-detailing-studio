The Process

Four Stages. No Shortcuts.

Correction is sequential — each stage has to be right before the next one means anything. This is the order we work in, every time.

STAGE 01

Decontamination

Iron removal, clay treatment, tar and bonded contaminant strip. Paint reset to a bare, honest surface before anything else happens.

STAGE 02

Paint Correction

Machine polishing to remove swirls, oxidation and RIDS, matched to your paint's hardness. Checked under raking light between passes.

STAGE 03

Ceramic Coating

A single, properly cured Nasiol system applied to corrected paint — chosen for your climate and how the car is actually driven.

STAGE 04

Maintenance

Coating-safe wash cycles and top-up schedules so the correction work lasts, instead of getting undone at the next wash.

FAQ

Common Questions

Do you really come to me?

Yes — we're fully mobile. We set up at your home, office or estate anywhere across Johannesburg. All we need is access to water and a power point near the vehicle.

How long does correction take?

Single-stage runs 2–4 hours. Multi-stage correction is 5–7 hours. Ceramic coating adds time on top and needs a 24-hour cure during which the car must stay dry.

What's the difference between one-step and two-step?

One-step is a single machine polish that removes light swirls and improves gloss. Two-step adds a heavy cut compound stage first, which removes deeper defects and RIDS. We measure your paint depth and tell you which one your car actually needs.

Do I need a deposit?

A 50% deposit secures the booking. The balance is due on completion. We'll confirm the full scope and a fixed price before any work starts.

Which coating should I choose?

Nasiol ZR53 gives 3+ years, NL272 gives 5+ years — both 10H. The right one depends on how the car is stored and driven. We'll tell you honestly rather than upsell the longest one.

Can you fix deep scratches?

Sometimes. If a scratch catches your fingernail it's usually through the clear coat, which correction can't fix without risking burn-through. We measure and tell you before touching it.

How do I pay?

EFT, cash, or card on site. You'll get a written quote with a reference number, and the same reference is used as your payment reference.
